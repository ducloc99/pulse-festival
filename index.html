Before You Go

Questions people actually ask.

When does the location get announced?

48 hours before gates open, sent by SMS and email to everyone with a confirmed ticket. GPS coordinates only, no address.

Is there phone signal on site?

Spotty at best in The Basin, none at all in The Static. Bring a paper map if you're meeting people — we're serious about this.

Can I bring my own tent?

Yes, in General and Basin Camp. The Ridge Pass includes pre-set canvas tents so you don't need to bring shelter at all.

Is there an age minimum?

18+, no exceptions, ID checked at gate against the name on your ticket. Transfers close two weeks before doors.

How do I get there without a car?

Shuttle buses run from three pickup points in the nearest city, departing every two hours from Thursday morning.

What if it's too hot?

Every stage and camp row has shade structures, and the Wellness Tent monitors heat conditions and adjusts set times if needed.
